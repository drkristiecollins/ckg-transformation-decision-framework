# CKG Transformation Decision Framework

**From possibility to evidence-based action.**

The CKG Transformation Decision Framework is a practical, evidence-based tool for evaluating business challenges and opportunities, selecting an appropriate path to transformation, and making decisions that support measurable business value.

## Purpose

Organizations can be tempted to jump directly to a solution—whether redesign, process improvement, automation, or artificial intelligence. This framework helps leaders pause, understand the business problem, examine the evidence, consider the consequences of action and inaction, and select a proportionate response.

## How the Framework Works

### 1. Apply the Three Ds

- **Data / Facts:** What do we know, and what evidence supports our understanding of the current state?
- **Demonstrate:** Can we demonstrate the issue, opportunity, or performance gap?
- **Demand:** What need, expectation, risk, or strategic priority creates a reason to act?

Distinguish the current evidence from what still needs to be established.

### 2. Evaluate Threats and Opportunities

Use the Threat/Opportunity Matrix to consider both maintaining the current state and pursuing change:

- **Threat — Don't Change**
- **Threat — Do Change**
- **Opportunity — Don't Change**
- **Opportunity — Do Change**

This makes the potential consequences of action and inaction visible.

### 3. Make Evidence Visible

Document sources, analysis, and key findings. Separate verified facts from assumptions and identify what evidence is still missing.

**Evidence strength is not the same as maturity.** A high or low rating describes the strength of the available evidence; it does not automatically indicate organizational maturity or readiness.

### 4. Select the Appropriate Transformation Path

Consider which response best fits the business problem and supporting evidence:

- **Redesign:** Change the underlying workflow, roles, handoffs, or operating model.
- **Continuous Improvement:** Address waste, variation, defects, delays, or rework.
- **Automation:** Streamline repeatable steps or handoffs with appropriate rules and controls.
- **Artificial Intelligence:** Consider AI when it offers meaningful advantages over simpler alternatives and feasibility, risk, governance, and human oversight have been addressed.

### 5. Make an Evidence-Based Decision

The framework supports five decision outcomes:

- **Proceed**
- **Proceed with Conditions**
- **Investigate Further**
- **Defer**
- **Stop**

Record the rationale, accountable owner, relevant conditions, and next action.

## Guiding Principle

**Time saved ≠ value created.**

Measure the outcome that matters to the business—and consider the effort required to sustain the improvement.
